PALL MALL DENTAL POLICIES & FAQ

--- CANCELLATION POLICY ---
We ask for at least 24 to 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Late cancellations or missed appointments may incur a fee.

--- NERVOUS PATIENTS ---
We warmly welcome nervous patients. Our team is highly experienced in putting you at ease, and we offer a relaxed environment with ceiling-mounted TVs. We take the time to explain every step of the treatment before we begin.

--- PARKING ---
There are multiple metered parking spaces directly outside the practice on Pall Mall. For extended visits, there are secure underground car parks within walking distance.

--- PUBLIC TRANSPORT ---
We are a few minutes' walk from Piccadilly Circus and Green Park tube stations, and a short stroll from Charing Cross station.

--- FINANCE ---
We offer 0% interest-free finance options for treatments. Invisalign financing starts from £37 per month, and Dental Implants from £58 per month. Please ask reception for details on how to apply.
