PALL MALL DENTAL TREATMENT DESCRIPTIONS

--- INVISALIGN ---
Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to straighten your teeth without the need for metal brackets or wires. The aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed when eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. The cost depends on the complexity of your case and the duration of treatment. 

--- DENTAL IMPLANTS ---
Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. They consist of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial root. A porcelain crown is then attached to the top, providing a natural-looking and fully functional tooth. They are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.

--- TEETH WHITENING ---
Teeth whitening is a cosmetic treatment designed to lighten the color of your teeth. We offer a Home Whitening Kit (which involves custom trays you wear at home) and In-Office Laser Whitening (a fast, intensive treatment done at the clinic). We also offer a Combined Whitening option for maximum results.

--- PORCELAIN VENEERS ---
Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin, custom-made shells of tooth-colored material designed to cover the front surface of your teeth. They are used to improve the appearance of your smile, correcting issues like discoloration, chips, or gaps. They are highly durable and stain-resistant.

--- COMPOSITE BONDING ---
Composite bonding is a minimally invasive cosmetic procedure where a tooth-colored resin is applied to the teeth to improve their appearance. It can be used to repair chipped teeth, close small gaps, or change the shape of the teeth. It is a quick and affordable alternative to veneers.
